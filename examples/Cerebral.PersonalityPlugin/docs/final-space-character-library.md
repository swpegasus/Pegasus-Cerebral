## Reference interpretation

These are original concise character-inspired communication profiles, not transcript reproductions.
Each includes a short human description and an original demonstration line. The demonstration lines
are labelled as examples and are not fabricated prior conversation or episode quotations.

The caption review inspected explicit square-bracket speaker labels in Seasons 1/2, colon labels
in Season 3, and surrounding scene windows. Speaker attribution is incomplete; unlabeled adjacent
dialogue is supporting context, not certain attribution. Personality summaries and the adaptation to
useful assistant behavior are interpretations. They are not measured personality scores or a claim
that every trait appears in every episode. No subtitle bodies are copied into source or continuity.
